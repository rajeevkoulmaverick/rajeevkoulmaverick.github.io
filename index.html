Live 24×7Bal Mukund

Handing an executive's inbox to an autonomous employee

Every inbox tool stops one step short — it summarises, then hands the work back to you. I built an agent that finishes the job. It wakes itself on a schedule on a $5 VPS, reads a 400,000-message mailbox, decides what a human must act on, writes real obligations to the calendar, and delivers one executive brief — then exits. Multi-LLM by design: the model is one swappable stage inside a deterministic pipeline, so a provider outage is a failover, not an outage.

Clinically advisedShield

Turning a consumer wearable into a cardiac early-warning system

A wearable records your heart and tells you almost nothing; a remote clinic owns an ECG machine and has nobody able to read it. Shield ingests 1-lead ECG straight from Apple Watch, Polar H10, Fitbit and Samsung Health, then runs a nine-condition arrhythmia classifier with Pan-Tompkins R-peak detection, HRV fingerprinting and recovery scoring, ICD-10 coded. The 12-lead extension reads a photographed printout and returns a triage call — built to over-refer when uncertain and to abstain outright when the image is unreadable.

0.902 mAUROC on PTB-XL across 23 diagnostic subclasses. Caught a VTach-suspect pattern at 200 bpm mid-workout and said stop.

Live 24×7Beacon

Reasoning about a family's safety from raw GPS

Safety is rarely an alarm button — it is a series of ordinary decisions, and the gap between “she is fine” and “I don't know” is where the worry lives. Phones stream raw fixes; server-side geofencing reasons over them continuously — accuracy-aware fence matching that refuses to fire on a kilometre-wide uncertainty circle, movement detection bracketed to real trips, stale-crossing suppression. The agents narrate rather than alarm: left college, reached the metro, on the way home. Silence is itself an alert.

Live context layered per person — nearest metro and minutes to the next train, peak-hour drive time, weather and AQI where they are.

LiveLean

Making health intelligence carry its own citations

Your watch says you burned 619 kcal. Then what? AI without evidence is confident noise. Lean runs engine-first: a sealed deterministic resolver handles food, workout and weight logs, and the LLM is invoked only when genuine reasoning is required — with real-time telemetry on where AI adds value against where rules suffice, and automatic failover across providers. Every number it reports carries a peer-reviewed citation; every estimate is flagged as one.

Predicts whether tomorrow's scale spike is water or fat. Fine-tuned on 12 years of personal Garmin data; current error 1.6%.
